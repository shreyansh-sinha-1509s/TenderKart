Project Overview

An intelligent and centralized platform for discovering, filtering and analyzing urban infrastructure tender opportunities.

Centralizing Infrastructure Opportunities

TenderKart is an academic project designed as a centralized platform for discovering and exploring urban infrastructure tender opportunities. It organizes opportunities into major infrastructure categories and provides users with a simple interface to search, filter and review relevant tender information.

The platform demonstrates how web technologies, databases, data analytics and intelligent information extraction can be combined to simplify the process of finding infrastructure opportunities.
