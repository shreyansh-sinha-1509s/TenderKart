How TenderKart Works

TenderKart simplifies urban infrastructure tender discovery, AI-assisted understanding, and personal bid application tracking across India.

🔍 Step 01

1. Discover infrastructure tender opportunities

Browse and search verified infrastructure notices by title, location, state, category, and tender status (Open, Closing Soon, Closed) across Roads, Bridges, Water Supply, Buildings, Metro Projects, and Smart City sectors.

✨ Step 02

2. Understand tender details using AI-assisted summaries

Open any tender details page to inspect its estimated budget, department, deadline, eligibility, and required documents — and click Generate AI Summary to condense lengthy specifications into clear bullet points.

🏛️ Step 03

3. Visit the official government portal to participate

Use the official government portal link provided on each tender to review official notices and submit your bid externally. Afterward, record your submission date and portal reference number in your Dashboard Application History to track progress.

ℹ️

Platform Scope & Disclaimer

TenderKart is an opportunity discovery and application tracking platform. Actual bid submission, document verification and procurement decisions are handled by the respective government authorities and their official portals.

Contractors

Discovery, Bookmarks & Application Tracking

Browse opportunities across India, bookmark tenders in your private Saved Tenders list, review AI-assisted summaries, and maintain your external portal submission history.

Central Admin

Centralized Tender Governance

Publish new infrastructure tenders, update tender details, close or reopen tenders based on deadlines, remove obsolete entries, and view live category distribution.
