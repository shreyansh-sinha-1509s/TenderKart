Platform Guidance & FAQs

How do contractors explore and apply for tenders?

Browse all 12 active urban infrastructure tenders across India in the Browse Tenders catalog, filter by state or category, generate AI digests, and record bids in your Contractor Dashboard.

How are tender inquiries handled?

Authenticated contractors select a specific tender and inquiry category. TenderKart automatically identifies the tender's state and routes the inquiry directly to the approved State Manager.

How do State Managers join the platform?

Department officers can register via the Manager registration tab by selecting their assigned Indian state. Accounts become active after Central Admin approval.
