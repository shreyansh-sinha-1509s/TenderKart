ABOUT TENDERKART

About TenderKart

Centralized urban infrastructure tender discovery, AI-assisted understanding, and personal application tracking.

Overview

What is TenderKart?

TenderKart is a centralized tender discovery and tracking platform designed to help contractors and businesses discover urban infrastructure tender opportunities more conveniently.

Background

Problem we address

Infrastructure tender information can be scattered across different sources. Finding suitable opportunities and keeping track of application details can be difficult.

Capabilities

Our solution

TenderKart helps users:

  • Discover infrastructure tenders.
  • Search by location and state.
  • Filter by category and other tender details.
  • Understand lengthy tender descriptions through AI-assisted summaries.
  • Save interesting tenders.
  • Record and track applications submitted through official procurement portals.

Who uses TenderKart?

Central Admin

Central Admin

Manages tender information — adding new tenders, updating details, controlling Open/Closed status, removing outdated notices, and reviewing category distribution.

Contractors

Contractors

Discover tenders across states and categories, bookmark relevant notices, review AI-assisted summaries, and maintain their own application records in the Dashboard.

ℹ️

Important disclaimer

TenderKart is a tender discovery and tracking platform. It is not a government e-procurement portal.

Actual bid submission, document verification, procurement decisions and tender awards take place through the relevant official government procurement portals and authorities.
